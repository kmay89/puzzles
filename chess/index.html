About this room

The Chess Room is part of The Solving Room — free things that teach, built plainly.

Everything runs in your browser: the rules engine (checked against millions of known-good positions), the 3D board (raw WebGL, no libraries), the coach, and the clock. Nearby play links two devices directly with WebRTC — the invite code is the connection; there is no server, no account, no tracking of any kind.

Install it (Add to Home Screen) and it works offline, and quietly tells you when a new version is ready.

MIT licensed. Be kind to your opponent — especially when you're winning.
